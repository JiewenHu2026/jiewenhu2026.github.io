About

Jiewen Hu is an internationally recognized landscape designer and researcher working across cultural landscapes, collective memory, and material expression. She explores how public spaces can give form to complex histories and foster healing, belonging, and shared experience.

Based in Philadelphia, Jiewen is a Landscape Designer II at OLIN. She holds a Master of Landscape Architecture from the University of Pennsylvania.

Her honors include two ASLA National Student Honor Awards, first place in the ASLA Emerging Professionals Design Competition, and a category win at the IFLA Global Landscape Architecture Awards. Her projects have also earned first place in World Landscape Architecture’s Design the World You Want competition, the Karl Foerster Commendation at the Peter Joseph Lenné Prize, and a win in the professional Conceptual Landscape category at the BLT Built Design Awards.

Her work has been featured by Landscape Architecture Magazine, ASLA LAND, World Landscape Architecture, Landezine, Penn Weitzman, Archinect, Urbanize LA, and Secret Los Angeles. Her projects have been exhibited in Berlin and as part of Melbourne Design Week. In 2025, she was invited to present From Ruins to Roots at the ASLA Conference on Landscape Architecture.
